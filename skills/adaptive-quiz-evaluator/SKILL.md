# Adaptive Quiz Evaluator Skill

## Overview
Creates self-assessment quizzes that diagnose knowledge gaps and reinforce retention through immediate constructive feedback.

## Operations
1. Generates multiple-choice, true/false, and conceptual short-answer questions.
2. Crafts plausible distractors reflecting common student misunderstandings.
3. Provides pedagogical rationales explaining why the correct choice is valid.
4. Calculates topical mastery scores to highlight areas requiring further study.
