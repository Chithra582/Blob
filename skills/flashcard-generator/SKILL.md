# Flashcard Generator Skill

## Overview
Generates effective active-recall flashcard decks adhering to cognitive science principles and the minimum-information rule.

## Operations
1. Formulates clear, unambiguous front-side query prompts.
2. Drafts concise, factually precise back-side answers.
3. Tags cards with difficulty level, subject category, and source topic.
4. Formats decks for seamless export to mobile app UI or Anki TSV/CSV formats.
