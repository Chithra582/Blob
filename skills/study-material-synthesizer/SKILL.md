# Study Material Synthesizer Skill

## Overview
Ingests raw, unstructured study material and distills it into foundational knowledge primitives, definitions, and thematic modules.

## Operations
1. Strips document formatting boilerplate and extracts core subject matter.
2. Identifies key definitions, formulas, chronological timelines, and conceptual rules.
3. Groups related ideas into modular 300-500 word conceptual chunks.
4. Flags ambiguous or contradictory statements for user clarification.
